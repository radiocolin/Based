New Standings tab & Scout Notebook Aesthetic Refinements

Standings:
- View MLB standings organized by league and division. Tap any team to jump to their full schedule.
- New "Standings" tab with pencil-styled numbered-list icon.
- Comprehensive team rows showing Rank, W-L, PCT, GB, L10, Streak, and Run Differential.
- Full VoiceOver support: headers announce league and division, cells read all team stats and context.

Scout Notebook Aesthetic Refinements:
- Organic Highlighter: Winning teams in the game list now feature a hand-drawn "marker" swipe. Using a multiply blend mode and intentional human inconsistency, the effect mimics physical ink soaking into the paper.
- Typographic Hierarchy: Standardized structural labels (headers, category tags, umpires) on IBM Plex Sans Condensed, while preserving the signature handwritten "pencil" font (Patrick Hand) for scores, players, and primary game data.
- Personalized Team Views: Team nicknames and season records now utilize team-specific brand colors (with automated contrast lifting for dark mode) to anchor each team's identity.
- Enhanced Schedule Layout: Status badges (NEXT, LIVE, PPD) now sit in a fixed gutter, ensuring team names stay perfectly aligned vertically for better scannability.
- Rescheduling Logic: Postponed games now feature a strikethrough on obsolete data (time, venue) with a "Resched" date, while moved games show their original date ("From: [Date]").
- UI Polish: Refined the win/loss bar labels and restored full-screen detent support for the Player Detail view.

What to test:
- Standings: Confirm correct division ranking, AL/NL header visibility, and accurate stats (GB, L10, Strk, RD).
- Game List: Verify the highlighter appears on winning teams in final games and looks organic and imperfect.
- Team View: Confirm the team name and record use the correct brand color and remain readable in both light and dark modes.
- Schedule: Check that team names align perfectly regardless of status badges (NEXT/LIVE/PPD).
- Rescheduling: Verify strikethrough on postponed games and the presence of "Resched" or "From" dates.
- Accessibility: Enable VoiceOver to verify that standings and schedule information are announced correctly.
- Theme Consistency: Confirm the mix of IBM Plex (structural) and Patrick Hand (data) fonts feels natural across all views.
- Team Navigation: Verify that tapping a team name on the Scoreboard (in Game View) correctly navigates to that team's full schedule.
- Favorites Management: Long-press any team in the Teams list, Schedule cards, or Standings to access the favorite/unfavorite context menu. Confirm the haptic feedback pulse when the menu appears.
- Team Roster: On any team detail page, use the pencil-styled "Schedule/Roster" picker to view the current active roster grouped by position. Verify that tapping a player opens their profile.
