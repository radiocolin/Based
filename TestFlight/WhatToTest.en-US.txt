New Standings tab

View MLB standings organized by league and division. Tap any team to jump to their full schedule.

- New "Standings" tab with pencil-styled numbered-list icon
- League headers ("American League", "National League") with pencil divider lines
- 6 division sections (East, Central, West) grouped under each league
- Each team row shows rank, W-L record, winning percentage, games back, last 10, streak, and run differential
- Tapping a team navigates to the team schedule view with full stats header
- Full VoiceOver support: headers announce league and division, cells read all stats
