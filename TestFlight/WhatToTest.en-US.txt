New in this build:

Scorecard fix: on the compact grid, extra-innings' automatic runner (the "Z" entry, placed on base to start the inning) used to spawn its own stray column — usually because that runner is the same batter who made the last out of the previous half-inning — permanently misaligning that batter's later at-bats from the rest of the lineup. It's now folded into that batter's previous at-bat cell as a small "Z" marker on second base instead.

-----

New in version 1.5.1, previous build:

Standings fix: Wild Card standings now sort by win percentage. Teams leading (or tied for) a Wild Card spot were sorting to the bottom of the list, below teams with far worse records, because of how their games-back value was being parsed.

WPBL scorecard fix: plays that previously showed as a plain "OUT" (WPBL tags plenty of routine batted-ball outs this way instead of a specific groundout/flyout/etc.) now show the actual fielding notation, e.g. "6-3" or "1-3", like the rest of the scorecard.

WPBL substitutions: pinch hitters, pinch runners, and defensive/pitching changes are now tracked in the lineup and scorecard grid — substitutes previously never appeared in the lineup at all, which also meant their at-bats were silently missing from team totals. Please flag anything that looks off around mid-game lineup changes, especially double-switches or a player re-entering later in the game.

Scorecard fix: on the compact grid, a substitute who entered the game but never got an at-bat used to show diamonds for their entire row instead of the grayed-out cells before their entry. Those cells are now correctly grayed out.

MLB scorecard fix: a runner thrown out on the bases while the next batter was still finishing their turn (e.g. an overturned replay review, or a caught stealing that ends the half-inning) could get attached to that batter's own at-bat — including a misleading "RUN" symbol and a description about the wrong players. These plays no longer show up under the wrong batter.

Scorecard fallback: any play type the app doesn't have a specific symbol for now shows a plain dash in the box instead of odd truncated text, with a short note added to the play's description explaining why.

-----

New in version 1.5, previous build:

WPBL fixes: extra-innings games now render the automatic runner placed on base to start the inning as its own "Z" entry, the same way MLB's does, instead of an unexplained "UNK". Games that go from scheduled to live no longer show up twice on the Games tab.

Home Run Grid polish: each player is now labeled with their position, and the card's typography and date now match the rest of the app more closely.

-----

New in version 1.5:

Initial support for the WPBL (Women's Pro Baseball League) — switch leagues from the dropdown on Games, Teams, and standings, and favorite WPBL teams alongside your MLB favorites in Settings. WPBL scorecards infer base-running from the league's play-by-play data, so please flag anything that looks off, especially base paths and pitch sequences.

New Home Run Grid export: from a game's scorecard, generate a condensed 9:16 share card with both full lineups side by side and home runs lit up.

Miscellaneous fixes: WPBL team colors, scorecard base-path rendering, and Teams/Standings navigation.
