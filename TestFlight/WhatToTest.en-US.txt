New Standings Tab & Aesthetic Refinements

Standings:
- View MLB rankings by league/division with detailed stats (Rank, GB, L10, RD).
- Full VoiceOver support for all team data and headers.

Scout Notebook Aesthetic:
- Organic Highlighter: Hand-drawn marker effect for winning teams.
- Typography: Improved hierarchy using IBM Plex Sans Condensed for structure and Patrick Hand for pencil data.
- Team Identity: Nicknames and records now use team brand colors.
- Scorecard: Organic pencil lines for pitching changes and "replaces [Pitcher]" labels in at-bat details.

Enhanced Features:
- Team Roster: View active rosters grouped by position with player profile links.
- Schedule: Improved vertical alignment and clear rescheduling (strikethrough/From) indicators.
- Navigation: Tap team names or long-press for favorites with haptic feedback.
